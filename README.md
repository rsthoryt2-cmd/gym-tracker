# 🏋️‍♂️ PPLUL Iron Gym Tracker

A progressive overload gym & lift tracking Progressive Web App (PWA) built specifically for the **PPLUL** split (Push, Pull, Legs, Rest, Upper, Lower, Rest).

Live on GitHub Pages: **[https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/](https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/)**

---

## ✨ Features

- **PPLUL 7-Day Split**: Pre-configured routines for Push (Mon), Pull (Tue), Legs (Wed), Rest (Thu), Upper (Fri), Lower (Sat), and Rest (Sun).
- **Previous-Session Memory**: Displays your exact weight & reps from the previous session for instant target recall.
- **Dynamic Adaptive Workouts**: Omitted lifts disappear from the next session; newly added exercises persist automatically.
- **Master Exercise Library**: Instant live search by exercise name or muscle group (Chest, Back, Shoulders, Legs, Arms).
- **Rest Timer**: Floating gym rest timer with quick 60s, 90s, and 120s presets.
- **Visual Progression & PRs**: Interactive SVG charts tracking load, estimated 1RM, and reps over time.
- **Excel Spreadsheet Export**: 1-click export of the past 30 days of workouts directly to `.csv` format (with UTF-8 BOM for Microsoft Excel & Google Sheets).
- **100% Offline Capable**: Works without internet at the gym. All data persists locally in device storage.

## 📱 How to Install on iPhone (iOS)

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Go to **[https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/](https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/)**.
3. Tap the **Share icon** (`⎋` / `⬆`) at the bottom of Safari.
4. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. Tap **Add**. The app will be placed directly on your iPhone home screen and launches full screen without any Safari address bar.

---

## 🤖 How to Install on Android

- **Option 1 (PWA)**: Open the link in Chrome, tap the `⋮` menu, and tap **"Install App"**.
- **Option 2 (Standalone APK)**: Download and install the pre-compiled [`pplul-tracker.apk`](./pplul-tracker.apk) directly on your device.
