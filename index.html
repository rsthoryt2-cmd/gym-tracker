🍎 Install on iPhone (iOS)

Apple does not allow outside APK or sideloaded apps, but iOS has official built-in support to install this directly from Safari as a native full-screen app:

  1. Open Safari: Open the default Safari browser on your iPhone (it must be Safari, not Chrome, because Apple only gives Home Screen install rights to Safari).
  2. Open the App: Open https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/ or scan the QR code in the Settings tab with your iPhone Camera.
  3. Tap Share Button: Tap the Share icon at the bottom center of Safari (the square box with an arrow pointing up ⎋ / ⬆).
  4. Add to Home Screen: Scroll down the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen" (icon with a plus sign ⊞).
  5. Confirm Add: The app name will show "PPLUL Iron" with the custom gym icon. Tap Add in the top right corner.
  6. Launch Fullscreen: Open the new Iron PPLUL app from your iPhone home screen! It opens completely full screen with no Safari browser bars, works 100% offline at the gym, and saves all your workouts directly inside your iPhone.

Install on Android Phone

You can run this as a 100% native-feeling app on your mobile phone without needing an app store:

  1. Open Chrome or Brave on your phone.
  2. Go to: https://rsthoryt2-cmd.github.io/gym-tracker/
  3. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner of Chrome.
  4. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home screen".
  5. Tap Install. The app icon will appear directly on your Android home screen and app drawer!
  6. It opens full-screen without any browser borders, works 100% offline at the gym, and saves all data on your phone!

📦 Android APK Installation

A standalone Android APK installer (pplul-tracker.apk) is ready for direct installation on your mobile phone:

  1. Download: Tap the green "⬇ Download APK" button in Settings, or visit http://192.168.100.89/gym/pplul-tracker.apk in your phone's browser.
  2. Open: When the download finishes, tap the notification or open the file from your phone's Downloads folder.
  3. Permissions: If Android asks "Allow from this source" (Install Unknown Apps), toggle it to Allow.
  4. Install: Tap Install. The app will be placed directly on your phone's home screen with the Iron PPLUL icon!
  5. 100% Offline: Once installed, it runs completely offline with no internet needed, saving all your lifts, PRs, and notes locally.
